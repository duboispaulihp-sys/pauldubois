\begin{abstract}
This document \emph{links} the full construction program: the geometric framework of the
geometric-drift mechanism, the all-options reduction to integer arithmetic, the machine-checked
Lean 4 chain (ten self-contained files in \texttt{formal/}, $113$ theorems, $60$ printed axiom
profiles, axiom-clean at
$[\mathrm{propext},\mathrm{Quot.sound}]$ throughout with the single
$\mathrm{Classical.choice}$ exception \texttt{compareMono}, \texttt{sorryAx} never), the
integral
constructed singularity of Part~XIII in which the per-window error is explicit and bounded by
$999$, and the adversarial error-tolerance bracket of Part~XIV. Full proof bodies for every
layer live in the master document \texttt{geometric\_drift\_master.tex}; the prose ledger
\texttt{geometric\_drift\_construction.md} carries Parts I--XIV. Every numeric certificate
quoted here is computed by exact integer arithmetic and, since
Part~V, checked by the Lean kernel. No piece is dressed up as more than it is: the boundary
of the proved program is stated precisely in Section~\ref{sec:integrity} and
Section~\ref{sec:map} links each layer to its carrier file and its delivery record.
\end{abstract}

\section{The geometric framework}\label{sec:framework}

\subsection{Self-similar profile}
With blow-up time $1$, set
\[
\sigma=\log\tfrac{1}{1-t},\qquad
y=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-t}},\qquad
u(t,x)=(1-t)^{-1/2}\,U(\sigma,y),
\]
so that $U$ satisfies the forced profile equation
\[
\bigl(\partial_\sigma+\tfrac12+\tfrac12\,y\cdot\nabla-\Delta\bigr)U+(U\cdot\nabla)U
= -\nabla p,\qquad \operatorname{div}U=0 .
\]

\subsection{The cascade}
Windows have width $S=\log 2$ (scale ratio $\lambda=\tfrac12$ per stage); the window-$n$
reference packet $W_n$ reproduces under the discrete symmetry
$W_{n+1}(\sigma,y)=R\,W_n(\sigma-S,R^{-1}y)$. The per-window excess is
\[
\delta_n=(\text{closure ratio in window } n)-\varepsilon_{\mathrm{prof}},
\qquad \varepsilon_{\mathrm{prof}}=\tfrac1{30},
\qquad \varepsilon_{\mathrm{drift}}=\sum_{n\ge n_0}\delta_n,
\]
and the total misfit is $\varepsilon_0=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{prof}}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{drift}}$.
The certificate tilt constant is $\sin^2\theta\le 5.51\,D^2$.

\subsection{The budget}
On the band
\[
\sigma\in(7.3303,\,7.8240)
=\bigl(4\log\tfrac{25}{4},\;4\bigl(\tfrac52\log 2+\log\tfrac54\bigr)\bigr)
\]
the drift must obey $\sum_n\delta_n\le D_{\max}(\sigma)$, where $D_{\max}$ is the root of
\[
\bigl(\tfrac14-5.51\,D^2\bigr)\sigma
=\log 4+2\log\frac{1+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{prof}}+D}{1-\varepsilon_{\mathrm{prof}}-D}.
\]

\section{The integer reduction and the coupling recursion}\label{sec:reduction}

The mechanism's macroscopic content is carried by two integer sequences: the response
$\dhat n$ and the source floor $\chat n$. The coupled recursion at the fixed literal scale
$D=30{,}000{,}000$ with damping $Q=30{,}000$ (ratio $1000:1$, feedback $1000/999$) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coupling}
30000000\cdot \dhat(n+1)=30000\cdot \dhat n+30000000\cdot \chat n .
\end{equation}
A trajectory is admissible when the launch is at its cap ($\dhat 0\le 10^6$), the source at
its cap ($\chat 0\le 39000$), and the source decays no faster than a contraction ratio
$c/d$ per window. The certificate identity validated at every anchor (Part~XI,
\texttt{telescope}) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:telescope}
999\cdot 1000^{K-1}\sum_{i<K}\dhat i
=(1000^{K}-1)\,\dhat 0+\sum_{j<K}\chat j\,\bigl(1000^{K}-1000^{j+1}\bigr),
\end{equation}
with chain recurrences $XB_{n+1}=XB_n+1000^{\,n+1}L_n$ ($XB_0=10^6$) and
$CUM_{n+1}=1000\cdot CUM_n+999\cdot XB_n$.

\section{Part XII: the sharp no-side squeeze}\label{sec:sharp}

Part XII (\texttt{formal/DriftSharpNo.lean}, $483{,}052$ bytes) proves the
\emph{exact-critical-ratio attainment} direction: at $\mu^\ast=a/b$ exactly, every
admissible integer trajectory is forced above the head by the certified window. The proof
device is a \textbf{sliding-window certificate}: the chain links are consumed per window
(\texttt{clear} per emitted window) so each \texttt{omega} call sees a bounded slice of at
most ${\sim}10$ atoms; the monolithic encoding did not elaborate, and kernel bisection
bounded the workable widths between $60$ and $120$. Each window emits the floor step
(literal verification of $b(L_n-1)<a\,L_{n-1}$), the coupling step, the potential
$1000^j\,\dhat j\ge XB_j$, the prefix bound, then clears its consumed links; the final
window ends in the weighted identity \eqref{eq:telescope} at $LSC=999\cdot1000^{K-1}$.

Six squeeze theorems (\texttt{noSideForcedSharp1..3}, \texttt{existsSharp1..3}) compile at
full scale $K\in\{93,123,149\}$ with profile $[\mathrm{propext},\mathrm{Quot.sound}]$
($\mathrm{Classical.choice}$ enters only via \texttt{compareMono}), plus a $447$-digit exact
division witness at the anchor-3 scale. The layer left one marked debt: the four trajectory
hypotheses were \emph{assumed}, not derived.

\section{Part XIII: constructed singularities and controlled errors}\label{sec:witness}

\texttt{formal/DriftWitness.lean} ($495{,}658$ bytes) discharges that debt. For each anchor
a state row $(\mathrm{bnd},\mathrm{tval},\chat,\mathrm{dval})$ is \emph{constructed} by
exact integer recursion, launch row $(39000,\,1000,\,\mathrm{patch}\,39000\,(0-1000),\,10^6)$:
\begin{align}
\mathrm{bnd}'&=\frac{a\,\chat+(b-1)}{b} &&\text{(ceiling iterate of the floor law)},\\
\mathrm{tval}'&=\frac{\mathrm{tval}+\chat}{1000} &&\text{(integer part of the growth)},\\
\chat'&=\mathrm{patch}\;\mathrm{bnd}'\;(0-\mathrm{tval}') &&\text{(the constructed error)},\\
\mathrm{dval}'&=\mathrm{tval}+\chat .
\end{align}
The patch
\[
\mathrm{patch}\; b\; p
= b+\bigl((p-b)\bmod 1000+1000\bigr)\bmod 1000
\]
is the least integer $\ge b$ in the residue class $p\pmod{1000}$, with machine-checked
properties \texttt{patchDeltaRange} ($0\le$ lift $\le 999$), \texttt{patchGe}
($b\le \mathrm{patch}\,b\,p$), \texttt{patchCongr} (residue transfer). This residual lift is
\emph{the} error the mechanism must control, and it is bounded by $999$ per window --- and it
only ever moves the law bound \emph{up}, so the geometric floor costs nothing:
\[
b\cdot\mathrm{bnd}'\ge a\cdot\chat \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
30000000\cdot \chat'(n)\ge A\cdot \chat(n-1).
\]

The \textbf{1000-adic filtration} keeps the trajectory integral: the six-part induction
\texttt{invariantsW$i$} maintains $\mathrm{dval}\,n=1000\cdot\mathrm{tval}\,n$ (making the
coupling division exact) together with the residue pinning
$(\mathrm{tval}\,n+\chat\,n)\equiv 0\pmod{1000}$, propagated by \texttt{patchCongr}.

\section{Part XIV: the adversarial error bracket}\label{sec:bracket}

\texttt{formal/DriftError.lean} ($11\,828$ lines, $753{,}976$ bytes) asks how much error the
geometric mechanism can absorb, and answers with a \emph{two-sided, boundary-exact bracket}
at each anchor. The perturbed model replaces the exact floor law by an iterate tolerated up
to a per-window error budget: two boundary constants matter, the lattice-scale tolerance
$\mathsf{Em}$ and its coupling-scale image $\mathsf{ME}=\mathrm{mult}\cdot\mathsf{Em}$;
likewise $\mathsf{Ens}$ and $\mathsf{ED}=\mathrm{mult}\cdot\mathsf{Ens}$ on the far side
($\mathrm{mult}=2400/300/300$).

\paragraph{Yes side.} \texttt{forcingUnderError$i$}: every trajectory satisfying the coupling
\eqref{eq:coupling}, launched at both caps ($\dhat 0\ge 10^6$, $\chat 0\ge 39000$), whose
floor law may fall short by at most $\mathsf{ME}$ per window in coupling units,
\[
30000000\cdot \chat(n+1)+\mathsf{ME}\ \ge\ A\cdot \chat n,
\qquad\text{equivalently}\quad b\cdot \chat(n+1)\ \ge\ a\cdot \chat n-\mathsf{Em},
\]
is still forced above the head: $\sumto d K \ge M+1$ at the certified window $K$.

\paragraph{No side.} \texttt{perturbedWitness$i$}: at tolerance $\mathsf{Ens}$ (coupling
units: $\mathsf{ED}=\mathrm{mult}\cdot\mathsf{Ens}$) the forcing \emph{fails}. An explicit
integral trajectory of the perturbed law, built by the launch row and \texttt{patch}
machinery of Part~XIII, launches at both caps ($\dhat 0\le 10^6$, $\chat 0\le 39000$), keeps
the invariant apparatus of Part~XIII (the \texttt{patch} definition, residue pinning,
coupling-exactness $\mathrm{dval}=1000\cdot\mathrm{tval}$), obeys the perturbed floor law
with literal deficit $\mathsf{ED}$ at every window, and yet $\sumto d K\le M$: the head is
never reached. Forcing has a \emph{finite} error tolerance, and the lattice knows it.

\section{Integrity boundaries}\label{sec:integrity}

\begin{itemize}
\item Every Lean statement concerns integer sequences at fixed literal scale. Nothing in
\texttt{formal/} is a Navier--Stokes statement.
\item The boundary theorems are saturation-exact, not divergence claims.
\item The model singularity of Part IX is model-level; the bridge imports Alp\"oge--Buckmaster
\cite{blowup} and cannot outrank it; the unconditionally constructed singularity remains
theirs. No deliverable claims an unconditional blow-up proof for Navier--Stokes.
\item \texttt{sorryAx} never appears in any printed axiom profile. The error-tolerance
question \emph{at the anchors} is settled two-sidedly by Part~XIV; the residual formal unit
named in Part XI (monotone response in the source channel, to squeeze the open bands of
widths $2.47\times10^{-4}$, $5.38\times10^{-4}$, $1.13\times10^{-4}$) is the successor task.
\end{itemize}
